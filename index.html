Frequently asked questions
📄 Do I need a visa to rent long-term in Italy?
Generally, non-EU citizens need a valid permit (residence or visa). I'll guide you based on your specific nationality and situation.

Within 24-48 hours, I provide the exact documents and steps. You can often get it within a week, sometimes same day at the agency.

Not at all. I offer remote consultations worldwide and have experience across Italy – Rome, Tuscany, Lombardy, and more.
