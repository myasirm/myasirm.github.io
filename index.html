My Story

Italian Haven Consultation was born from a simple yet powerful idea: to make life easier for foreigners who dream of living in Italy but struggle to find the right guidance.

My name is Yasir. Originally from Pakistan, I was raised and educated in the United Kingdom, graduating from Coventry University. After completing my studies, I moved to Italy, where I have been living and working for over 15 years.

Over the years, I’ve helped many individuals and families navigate immigration, property, and settling into Italian life.
