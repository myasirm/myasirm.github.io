About Italian Haven

My Story

Italian Haven Consultation was born from a simple yet powerful idea: to make life easier for foreigners who dream of living in Italy but struggle to find the right guidance.

My name is Yasir. Originally from Pakistan, I was raised and educated in the United Kingdom, graduating from Coventry University.

After completing my studies, I moved to Italy, where I have been living and working for over 15 years. Through my experience, I gained deep insight into Italian lifestyle, property, and client services.

Over the years, I met many individuals and families facing the same challenges I once had — navigating bureaucracy, finding the right home, and adapting to a new culture.

That’s why I created Italian Haven Consultation — to offer real, honest, and practical support based on experience, not theory.
