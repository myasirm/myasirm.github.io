From immigrant to expert: the story behind Italian Haven
Real empathy, deep knowledge, and 15+ years of lived experience.

Ciao, I'm Yasir.

Originally from Pakistan, raised and educated in the United Kingdom — graduating from Coventry University — I always felt a magnetic pull toward Italy. After moving here, I spent over 15 years living and working across different Italian regions, from bustling Rome to the serene hills of Tuscany, and now proudly based in Puglia.

Throughout this journey, I witnessed countless foreigners struggle with Italian bureaucracy, housing hurdles, and cultural adaptation — exactly the same obstacles I faced at the beginning. That frustration fueled my mission: Italian Haven Consultation was born to offer honest, practical, and experienced-based guidance. No sugar-coating, no vague theories — just real solutions.

We help you transform your Italian dream into a secure, joyful reality.
